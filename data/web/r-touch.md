The Only Robotic Rehabilitation for Both Hand & Arm
Experience complete upper limb recovery with R-TOUCH! The perfect-fit soft robotic glove offers training from passive to active hand movements, while the sensory glove enables mirror therapy for bilateral activation.
The arm is precisely tracked with an optical sensor and supported by dynamic arm support, providing adjustable weight relief of up to 5 kg.
Highest Level of Precision
R-TOUCH achieves unmatched movement accuracy through its unique string system with 5 finger actuators, precisely guiding each finger’s movement for optimal rehabilitation results.
Fully customizable movement parameters, including range of motion, starting position, and speed, allow therapists to tailor every exercise to the patient’s needs. All performance data is automatically recorded in detailed reports for progress tracking and outcome evaluation.
Suitable for Children and Adults
Not only complete upper limb rehabilitation, but also unmatched adaptability, R-TOUCH fits every patient, from children to adults.
The versatility of the robotic hand glove keeps patients progressing at every stage of neurorehabilitation. It supports recovery from the very first attempts to activate movement all the way to advanced training that builds precise and complex motor skills.

Rehabilitation For A Broad Range Of Patients
Stroke patients
R-TOUCH promotes real-life functionality for stroke patients through its unique setup: a soft robotic glove that leaves the palm free for grasping, paired with an adjustable table and arm support. This enables patients to practice meaningful tasks with real objects and regain independence.
Patients with spasticity
Thanks to fully customizable therapy modes and dedicated accessories, our rehabilitation robot glove can be used safely and effectively even for patients with spasticity, a common challenge in neurological rehabilitation.
Pediatric patients
R-TOUCH helps pediatric patients with cerebral palsy improve hand and arm coordination, with multiple glove sizes that allow the device to be adapted to each child. Interactive exercises keep them engaged and motivated throughout therapy.
