Autonomous
Natural Walking
To replicate a natural walking experience, our powered exoskeleton uses a unique weight shifting mechanism to trigger each step, allowing for smooth and intuitive movement. Together with a custom-fit trunk support for better upper body stability, it enhances walking confidence by enabling more controlled and secure movement.
By engaging all major lower limb joints – hip, knee, ankle, and metatarsal – BTL EXO ensures coordinated movement throughout the gait cycle, enabling more natural and effective gait re-education compared to other solutions.
Designed For
Any Environment
Designed for real-world use, BTL EXO allows walking on flat surface, uneven terrain, and various inclines – all while maintaining control, ensuring stability and minimizing fall risk.
Unlike conventional systems limited to clinical settings, our versatile exoskeleton robotic suit enables functional mobility training in conditions that closely reflect everyday challenges.
Unmatched Setup Speed
BTL EXO ensures an optimal fit in just a few minutes.
Thanks to a hip-release mechanism that enables easy entry and exit, as well as quick-release pins for fast lower limb size adjustments, you spend less time preparing your patients and have more focus on the actual therapy – a clear advantage over more complex or time-consuming alternatives.
