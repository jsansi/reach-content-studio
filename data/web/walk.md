Unique EMG-Driven Biofeedback
In the acute phase of gait rehabilitation, BTL WALK helps reduce neurological symptoms such as pain and swelling, reduces spasticity to improve mobility, and supports the strengthening of weakened muscles.
EMG-driven real-time biofeedback retrains correct muscle activation for functional walking. It also prepares patients for safe and effective walking, supports proper muscle activation, timing, and coordination, and builds the foundation for functional gait recovery.
Adaptive Gait Sensing
& Precise Stimulation
In the advanced phase, BTL WALK continuously adapts to the patient’s gait using integrated smart sensors, delivering precisely-timed functional electrical stimulation (FES) for natural and effective walking.
It transfers prepared gait patterns into daily walking and enables repetitive, task-specific practice in real-life environments. This supports long-term functional improvement and motor learning.

The Comprehensive Solution for Drop Foot
Drop foot following stroke
BTL’s device for drop foot helps stroke patients by activating the muscles responsible for lifting the foot during walking. This improves foot clearance, reduces the risk of tripping, and promotes a more natural and symmetrical gait.
Gait impairment in Traumatic Brain Injury
Our efficient FES stimulator supports correct muscle activation at the right time, enhances walking safety, and contributes to functional mobility recovery.
Maintaining walking ability in Multiple Sclerosis
BTL WALK helps compensate for fatigue-related muscle weakness and supports more efficient walking. It assists with foot lift during gait and enables patients to remain active and independent for longer during daily activities.
