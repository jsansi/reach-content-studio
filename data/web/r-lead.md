Innovative Solution
for Upper Limb Training
R-LEAD is a cutting-edge arm therapy machine for rehabilitation. It tracks and guides the limb in space through interactive on-screen exercises. During training, the device provides adjustable arm weight support, tailored to the individual needs of each patient.
It enables both active and assistive training, empowering patients to initiate movement independently or receive precisely guided support when needed.

Arm Rehabilitation
The arm is dynamically supported with up to 5 kg of adjustable weight relief. A precise optical sensor continuously tracks movement, enabling real-time on-screen feedback to guide patients during training.
All training data are automatically recorded in a patient database, enabling detailed progress tracking, objective evaluation, and long-term monitoring of each patient’s performance.
Cognitive Training
Our arm therapy machine R-LEAD combines active rehabilitation with cognitive training, driving faster, more effective, and lasting rehabilitation results.
Through proprioceptive exercises and real-life task simulations, patients train purposeful, functional movements. This helps enhance motor control, coordination, and cognitive engagement, which translate directly into everyday activities.
Interactive Gaming Therapy
No more boring routines, R-LEAD arm therapy brings a gamified experience with dozens of games that motivate and keep patients fully engaged in training.
R-LEAD features self-adapting difficulty levels that automatically match each patient’s progress. It also provides clear voice guidance throughout the exercises. Together, these features ensure personalized, intuitive, and immersive training.

Rehabilitation For A Broad Range Of Patients
Neurological patients
R-LEAD is designed to aid recovery from neurological conditions such as stroke, brain injury, and spinal cord injury. It helps patients regain movement, coordination, and functional use of the upper limb through targeted, customizable therapy, supporting effective rehab of the hand and upper extremity.
Orthopedic patients
R-LEAD supports arm and hand therapy for musculoskeletal disorders and post-operative recovery by promoting motor function, rebuilding strength, and improving mobility. Its adaptable exercises help patients gradually regain independence in daily activities with confidence and control.
Pediatric patients
R-LEAD provides engaging training for pediatric patients with neurological or musculoskeletal disorders, supporting coordinated arm and hand movements, improving motor control, and fostering functional upper limb development through interactive exercises tailored to each child’s needs.
