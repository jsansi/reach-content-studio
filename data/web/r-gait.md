Robotic Early Gait Recovery
Even in the earliest stages of recovery, our gait rehabilitation robot helps your patients take up to 1,500 steps in under 30 minutes, thanks to a lightning-fast patient setup that takes less than 5 minutes.
This high level of repetition supports faster gait re-learning and improved walking patterns, while the high-dose, task-specific training builds strength and coordination in ways conventional therapy simply cannot match.
The Most Natural
Gait Pattern
A unique combination of flexible footplates that actively flex the metatarsal joint, 3D pelvic motion combining vertical, horizontal, and rotational movements, and dynamic body-weight support perfectly synchronized with the rhythm of walking delivers the most natural gait experience.
The system accommodates a wide range of patients, supporting individuals with overweight up to 175 kg and children from 80 cm in height, ensuring that everyone can benefit from safe and physiological gait rehabilitation.
Interactive
Feedback-Driven Therapy
Motivational exercises engage patients at every step, turning each session into an interactive and rewarding experience that keeps them moving and improving.
At the same time, precise real-time feedback from 16 sensors embedded in the footplates empowers therapists to monitor progress and adjust training instantly.
All data is stored in the patient database and available for export and later analysis, supporting highly effective and personalized gait rehabilitation.

Support Across Neurological Conditions
Stroke
R-GAIT provides gait training for stroke patients and helps them regain walking ability through high-repetition, task-specific gait training that promotes neuroplasticity and accelerates motor recovery.
Traumatic Brain Injury
For patients with traumatic brain injury, R-GAIT provides safe, intensive gait training that rebuilds strength, coordination, and endurance.
Cerebral Palsy
R-GAIT supports children with cerebral palsy by promoting proper walking patterns and enhancing mobility through engaging, adaptive, and playful gait training making it an effective gait trainer for cerebral palsy.
Other neurological diseases
R-GAIT is also suitable for patients with Multiple sclerosis, Parkinson’s disease, and other neurological conditions that impact walking ability.
