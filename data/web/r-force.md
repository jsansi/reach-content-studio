The Only Zero Gravity Treadmill
R-FORCE is the only rehabilitation treadmill that enables active training with full body-weight support, using air pressure to reduce the patient’s weight by up to 100%.
Unweighting levels and key gait parameters can be adjusted during therapy, ensuring precise personalization to each patient’s needs. This adaptability supports progressive training and facilitates a smooth transition from highly assisted movement to independent gait.
Training Right After Injury
With adjustable unweighting, R-FORCE enables patients to begin walking or running immediately after injury, minimizing joint stress and supporting safe, effective, and more intensive rehabilitation from day one.
On our body weight supported treadmill, patients have the opportunity to learn balance control and proper movements through interactive exercises. These skills can then be seamlessly transferred into their gait training, enhancing early rehabilitation outcomes.
Provides Real-Time Therapy Feedback
An integrated wide-angle camera captures the walking sequence in real time, giving patients clear visual feedback to self-correct and improve with every step.
The system further combines the camera with treadmill-embedded sensors, enabling detailed tracking of gait parameters such as walking symmetry and weight distribution. All data are stored in comprehensive reports for analysis and progress monitoring.

Rehabilitation For A Broad Range Of Patients
Orthopedic recovery
R-FORCE helps orthopedic patients safely re-learn to load the impaired limb, improve joint stability, and correct gait patterns like step length, width, and symmetry. Zero-impact movement supports a gradual return to full weight-bearing and active mobility.
Neurological Rehabilitation
Ideal for patients with balance disorders, ataxia, or muscle weakness, BTL’s anti-gravity treadmill promotes safe movement through gradual unweighting, real-time feedback, and interactive exercises that enhance coordination, gait consistency, and postural control.
Sports Training
R-FORCE is ideal for sports injury recovery and sports rehabilitation. By offloading up to 100% of the patient’s body weight, it enables early re-training without overload and reduces joint stress. Real-time feedback and progressive loading support muscle strengthening and help improve sport performance.
