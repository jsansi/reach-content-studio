# BTL El Salvador — brand and editorial rules for slides

Source: BTL Corporate Identity v2.3 (knowledge/brand-guidelines/btl-corporate-identity.md) plus REACH agency rules.

## Format
- Feed carousel 1080×1350 (`Slide` default); square 1080×1080 and story 1080×1920 also available.
- Brand margin 2 U = 94 px from every edge (`"m"` in `Region`). U = half the logo height.

## Logo
- Exactly one `BtlLogo` per slide, 200 px wide, in a corner, 2 U from the edges.
- Keep its 1 U safe zone free of text, lines and image detail; it must sit on a plain area.
- White on dark/blue/enterprise backgrounds, Enterprise Blue on light/white. Never recolor, rotate, crop or add effects.
- A product wordmark (`ProductLogo`) goes at least 1 U away from the BTL logo.

## Color
- Enterprise Blue #00508C (primary), Dark Blue #000A32, Sky Blue #96D2FF, White.
- Gradients: radial, two brand colors, light source outside a corner (`Slide lightFrom`). On `blue`, keep the light away from the logo corner.
- No other colors on BTL Medical slides.

## Typography
- Noto Sans. Light for headlines and body, Thin for oversized numerals, SemiBold sparingly (display hooks, labels).
- Left-aligned, sentence case. Never highlight words inside a sentence: no color change, italics or underline within a text.
- Minimum 30 px for every text on the 1080 canvas, including "Desliza…", labels and footnotes; small text in Regular weight.

## Copy
- Brand in copy is always "BTL El Salvador" (never "BTL Medical" or "BTL Estética").
- CTA: "Solicita tu demo"; demos are requested by direct message with the word "DEMO".
- Numbers as "+100". Benefits for the clinic before technical specs.
- Hooks open a curiosity gap; the swipe cue ("Desliza…") goes on every slide except the last.
- BTL Spinal Decompression treats the lumbar AND cervical zones.

## Claims
- Never invent equipment, features, outcomes, testimonials or contacts.
- Figures and indications come from BTL documentation; they are manufacturer descriptions, not formally approved clinical claims. Show the source in a `Footnote`, and never promise results ("Los resultados pueden variar según cada paciente.").

## Imagery
- Only official BTL product photos (`spinalDecompressionImages`, or images supplied by BTL). Do not crop people's heads.
- Photos with white backgrounds blend into light slides automatically; on dark slides prefer the transparent ones (`semiincorporada`, `prono`).
- Icons: only the five Material Symbols Sharp weight-100 icons in `Icon`.
